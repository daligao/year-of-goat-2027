Frequently Asked Questions

What year is my Chinese zodiac sign?

Find your birth year in the chart above to see your animal sign. The 12 signs repeat in the same order every 12 years — Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog, Pig — so your sign also governed the years 12, 24, 36, 48, and 60 years before or after your birth year.

Why do some charts disagree on a year's sign?

Most disagreement comes from the Jan/Feb boundary — some simplified charts assume the zodiac year starts January 1, when it actually starts on the Chinese New Year date (which falls between January 21 and February 20). Anyone born before that year's Chinese New Year is technically still in the previous animal's year.

What if I was born in January or February?

If your birthday falls in January or early-to-mid February, this simplified year-based chart may not be accurate — you need the exact Chinese New Year date for your birth year to know for certain. Use the site's exact zodiac calculator, which handles the Spring Festival boundary precisely using your full birth date.

What zodiac sign is 2027?

2027 is the Year of the Goat — specifically a Fire Goat year, since the Chinese calendar also cycles through five elements alongside the 12 animals. It begins on Chinese New Year, February 6, 2027.
