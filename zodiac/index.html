Why Your Birth Month Matters

🗓 Chinese New Year changes every year

Chinese New Year (the start of a new zodiac year) falls on a different date each year — anywhere from January 21 to February 20. This means people born in January or early February need to check the exact CNY date for their birth year.

Example: Born February 3, 1990?
Chinese New Year 1990 was January 27, 1990.
→ You were born AFTER CNY, so you're a Horse (not Snake).

Born January 20, 1990?
→ You were born BEFORE CNY 1990, so you're a Snake (the previous year's sign).

🌿 BaZi vs. Popular Tradition

This calculator uses the popular Chinese New Year date. Traditional BaZi (八字) astrology uses the solar term Li Chun (立春, Start of Spring — around February 4) as the true year boundary. Our result shows the popular version; deep BaZi readings use Li Chun.
