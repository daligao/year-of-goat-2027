What Is the Year of the Fire Goat?

2027 is designated 丁未年 (Dīng Wèi Nián) in the Chinese sexagenary cycle — the Year of the Fire Goat. Chinese New Year falls on February 6, 2027, marking the official start of the new zodiac year. The year closes on January 25, 2028.

The Goat (未, Wèi) is the 8th animal in the 12-year Chinese zodiac cycle, and 2027's ruling element is Yin Fire (丁) — a soft, steady flame rather than a roaring blaze. Think candlelight rather than wildfire: creative, warm, emotionally intelligent, and oriented toward beauty and community.

🔥 What "Fire Goat" means

In Chinese metaphysics, every zodiac year carries both an animal sign and a Heavenly Stem element. The stem 丁 (Dīng) brings Yin Fire — associated with artistic expression, spiritual reflection, and gentle determination. Combined with the Goat's Earth nature, 2027 favours collaborative work, creative projects, and nurturing relationships over aggressive competition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What year is 2027 in the Chinese zodiac?

2027 is the Year of the Goat — specifically the Fire Goat (丁未年, Dīng Wèi Nián). Chinese New Year 2027 begins on February 6, 2027 and ends on January 25, 2028.

What does Ding Wei (丁未) mean?

丁 (Dīng) is the 4th Heavenly Stem, representing Yin Fire — a gentle, candle-like flame associated with warmth, creativity and intuition. 未 (Wèi) is the 8th Earthly Branch, corresponding to the Goat. Together they describe a year of creative warmth, artistic energy, and harmonious community focus.

Which zodiac signs do best in 2027?

Rabbit, Horse and Pig benefit most. Rabbit and Pig form the San He (三合) harmony triad with the Goat. Horse shares the Liu He (六合) perfect-pair bond — the strongest 1-on-1 zodiac relationship. Tiger and Snake also see above-average fortune.

Is 2027 good for the Goat sign itself?

Goats are in their Ben Ming Nian (本命年) — their own birth-sign year. Traditional belief considers this both significant and vulnerable. It is not necessarily bad, but it calls for extra caution: avoid major financial risks, wear red accessories for protection, and focus on consolidating existing gains rather than launching bold new ventures.
