Everything You Need to Know About 2027 Chinese New Year

When Is Chinese New Year 2027?

Chinese New Year 2027 falls on Saturday, February 6, 2027. This date marks the first day of the first lunar month in the Chinese calendar. The celebration period typically runs for 15 days, culminating in the Lantern Festival on February 20, 2027.

The Chinese New Year date changes each year because it follows the lunisolar calendar, which means it moves relative to the Gregorian calendar. In 2026, Chinese New Year was on January 17. In 2028, it will shift to January 26.

Why Is 2027 the Year of the Goat?

The Chinese zodiac rotates through 12 animals in a fixed sequence. After the Year of the Horse (2026), the cycle moves to the Goat. 2027 is specifically the Fire Goat year (丁未年, Dīng Wèi Nián), combining the Goat animal with the Fire element in the traditional heavenly stems and earthly branches system.

The Goat (also rendered as Ram or Sheep in some traditions) is the 8th sign of the Chinese zodiac. It carries associations with creativity, gentleness, empathy, and artistic sensibility. Fire Goat years historically bring a blend of warmth, generosity, and the need to balance creativity with practicality.

How to Say Happy Chinese New Year in Chinese

新年快乐 — Xīnnián kuàilè — Happy New Year (everyday use)

恭喜发财 — Gōngxǐ fācái — Wishing you prosperity (Cantonese: Gung Hei Fat Choi)

羊年大吉 — Yángnián dàjí — Great luck in the Year of the Goat

万事如意 — Wànshì rúyì — May all go as you wish

身体健康 — Shēntǐ jiànkāng — Good health to you

How to Choose a Chinese New Year Greeting

The right Chinese New Year greeting depends on your relationship and context:

  • For friends: Warm, casual, personal. You can add humor or inside references. A short blessing is often more genuine than a formal long one.
  • For family: Focus on health, togetherness, and gratitude. Elders particularly appreciate traditional phrases and expressions of respect.
  • For clients and coworkers: Keep it professional and sincere. Avoid humor unless you know the person well. 'Wishing you and your team a prosperous New Year' is always appropriate.
  • For a partner: Personal, warm, and specific to your relationship. This is the one greeting where referencing private jokes or shared history is exactly right.
  • For children: Fun, energetic, enthusiastic. Kids respond to warmth and excitement. Red envelopes (hongbao) traditionally accompany verbal greetings for children.

Chinese New Year Greeting Etiquette

A few things worth knowing before you send:

  • Send greetings within the 15-day celebration period (February 6–20, 2027). Sending early is also acceptable and often appreciated.
  • The first day is most important for close family. The 15th day (Lantern Festival) is a secondary celebration often marked with friends.
  • Red is the lucky color — a red greeting card or red background image carries positive cultural meaning.
  • Avoid references to death, illness, debt, or bad fortune in any greeting — even in passing.
  • For Cantonese-speaking contacts, 恭喜发財 (Gung Hei Fat Choi) is widely used. For Mandarin-speaking contacts, 新年快乐 is more common.
