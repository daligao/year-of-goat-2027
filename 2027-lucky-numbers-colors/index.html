Lucky Numbers & Colors for 2027

The Goat's traditional lucky numbers, colors, and what's considered unlucky — plus where this tradition comes from.

Lucky Numbers: 2 and 7

In Chinese numerology, each of the 12 zodiac signs carries its own set of traditional lucky numbers. For the Goat — the governing sign of 2027 — the numbers 2 and 7 are most commonly cited.

2

Pairs & Harmony

Associated with partnership and balance — fitting for the Goat's cooperative, relationship-oriented nature.

7

Completion

A number often linked with introspection and steady progress, echoing the Goat's thoughtful, artistic temperament.

Lucky Colors: Green, Red, Purple

Green

Linked to the Wood element, nature, and pastures — a natural fit for the grazing, gentle Goat.

Red

The universal Chinese color of good fortune, worn widely at Chinese New Year regardless of sign.

Purple

Traditionally tied to nobility and refinement, echoing the Goat's artistic and cultured reputation.

Traditionally Considered Unlucky

Numbers to avoid: 6 and 9

Some traditions list 6 and 9 as the Goat's less favorable numbers — though this varies by source and region, and is treated as folk custom rather than firm rule.

Colors to avoid: Dark blue and black

Dark blue and black are sometimes listed as colors to avoid for Goat-year wear, seen as clashing with the year's warmer, brighter associations. Again, this is cultural custom, not a strict prohibition.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are lucky numbers the same for everyone in 2027?

The Goat year's general lucky numbers (2, 7) and colors (green, red, purple) are traditionally associated with the year itself and are commonly cited for anyone. However, some practitioners also calculate personal lucky numbers based on an individual's own birth-year zodiac sign and BaZi chart, which can differ from the year's general associations.

Why green for the Goat?

Green is traditionally linked to the Wood element and to nature, growth, and pastures — fitting for the Goat, an animal associated with grazing, gentleness, and the natural world. It's commonly paired with the Goat sign in folk tradition, alongside red (general good fortune) and purple (nobility and refinement, echoing the Goat's artistic reputation).

Do lucky numbers change by zodiac sign?

Yes. Chinese numerology traditionally assigns different lucky numbers to each of the 12 zodiac signs, not just to the year as a whole. The numbers listed on this page (2 and 7) are specifically the Goat's traditional lucky numbers, which is why they're highlighted for the 2027 Goat year.

Is this superstition or tradition?

Lucky numbers and colors come from centuries-old Chinese folk tradition rather than any scientific claim. Many people treat them as cultural custom — similar to wearing green on St. Patrick's Day — rather than a literal prediction. They're widely referenced around Chinese New Year in decor, gifts, and clothing choices.
